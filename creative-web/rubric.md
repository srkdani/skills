# Rubric

Score after you have seen a thumbnail about 300px wide and a 10 second scroll. Be harsh. A costume page does not get points for being busy.

Thumbnail:

- T1 Guess the business, text blurred or ignored. 0 no, 1 vague, 2 yes.
- T2 Swap test. If a competitor's name replaced the brand, would the page still work? 0 yes fully, 1 partly, 2 no, it only fits this brand.
- T3 Template. 0 template or costume, 1 unsure, 2 made for this brand. Costume means particles, ring cursor, fade-up everywhere, black plus acid, cream plus italic serif, glass, or numbered sections. Also 0 if sections repeat heading, text, and cards.
- T4 Memory. 0 nothing, 2 a specific image or shape (write it down). No 1.

Scroll:

- R1 Story. 0 boxes stacking, 1 some connection, 2 one object, world, or journey.
- R2 Surprise. 0 none, 1 mild, 2 a clear oh or a smile (write what).
- R3 About the brand. 0 generic motion (bobbing icons, fade-up, a loop that could sit on any site), 1 loosely related, 2 the moving parts enact a brand truth.
- R4 Control, a floor. 2 if you never wait, feel stuck, or lose your place. 0 if you do. Any wait is a floor failure.
- R5 Readable, a floor. 2 if, within the first screen, you can say what the business is, what to do, and one practical fact. 0 if the only labels are cryptic or the action is missing.

Creative score is T1 + T2 + T3 + T4 + R1 + R2 + R3, maximum 14. Floor is R4 + R5, and it must be 4.

A page is done only if the creative score is at least 10, the floor is 4, and the budget is complete: at least 5 small details, 2 or 3 medium interactions, and 1 big moment, each tied to a truth. One picture grade. Against a plain or costume baseline, the gap should be obvious in the thumbnail.

After scoring, write one sentence: the idea of the site. If that sentence does not match the concept in the plan, the page drifted. Rebuild.
