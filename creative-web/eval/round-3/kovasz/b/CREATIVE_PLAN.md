# Kovász, sample

Deal salt r3a. World kept: The verb. Motion kept: Spring. Picture kept: Material swatch.

## Harvest

Facts only this bakery has, or only in this form. Physical and sensory items are marked.

1. The starter is 41 years old.
2. It was brought from the baker's grandmother's village.
3. The oven is lit at 11pm and the bake runs overnight.
4. The door opens at 6am.
5. The loaves are gone by noon. Nothing is baked for the afternoon.
6. The shop is in Budapest.
7. Kovász is the Hungarian word for the starter itself. The shop is named for the jar, not for a style of loaf.
8. Physical: proved dough springs back once when a thumb lets go. Bakers call that the poke.
9. Physical: the lame leaves a shut slit that opens in the oven and lifts one ear.
10. Physical: the crust crackles while it cools on the rack after the 6am pull.
11. Physical: 11pm smells like raw flour and the wet starter. 6am smells like hot crust in a cold street.
12. Physical: steam in the first minutes of the bake blisters the skin.
13. Physical: the peel and the board carry a pale flour film. At noon the film is all that is left.
14. Physical: the starter well is wetter and sharper than the dough around it.
15. Physical: before dawn the street side of the room is cold and the oven side is hot.
16. The night produces one bake. There is no second shift after noon.

None of these is a virtue word. Items 8 through 15 are physical or sensory.

## Truths

Truth 1. The starter is 41 years old and came from a grandmother's village, and the shop is named for that jar. Rival: a new city sourdough counter that ordered a dried culture. They do not have this jar, and their name is not the word for the starter.

Truth 2. The oven goes on at 11pm, the door opens at 6am, and the whole bake is that one night. Rival: a daytime bakery that mixes after sunrise and sells through the afternoon. They do not run the oven against the night.

Truth 3. The board is bare by noon because the night's bake is the only bake. Rival: a supermarket bakery that restocks from a freezer all day. They do not end when one rack cools.

## Emotion

Suspense.

"I slid the night along the peel, the loaf sprang open once, and by noon the board was only flour."

## Concepts

The site is a feeding crock tipped at 11pm, where forty-one years become the wet center and tipping starts the bake. Rival: a yogurt brand. They tip a culture, but the tip does not become one loaf that is gone by noon. The line breaks for them. Spare, not picked.

The site is an overnight oven spring, where the hours from 11pm to noon become one opening score and sliding the peel springs the loaf. Rival: a night-shift bagel shop. A bagel does not open a lame ear from a 41-year village starter, and that shop does not end at noon. The line breaks. Keep.

The site is a lame used as a pen, where the headline is a slash and dragging the blade letters the crust. Rival: another sourdough bakery. They also cut names into loaves. FAIL.

Replaced: The site is a noon cooling rack, where the night's loaves are heat-shadows in the shape of an opened score and taking the last shadow locks the board. Rival: a donut shop that sells out. Their rack is not an opened lame score from one 11pm bake. The line breaks. Spare, not picked.

## Pick

The overnight oven spring.

The visitor sentence comes true only if they move the night and see one loaf spring, then see flour where the loaf was. The crock would make them tip a jar. The rack alone would show emptiness without the spring. No second concept is added.

World rejected: Operable document. A bake log would make the hero a paper sheet. The picture rule says the hero is the dough itself.

World rejected: Worker in the room. A round baker beside the page would sit next to the material. The picture rule is a swatch of dough, not a character, and the footer would become a bed for that character.

Motion kept: Spring, `back.out(1.4)`, 0.35 to 0.6s, once. It matches the poke: the dough springs once and stops. Menus and age switches stay under 150ms. Nothing else bounces.

Picture kept: Material swatch. The hero is dough built from skin, flour, crumb, crust, and the score slit. No glow, no neon edge. Flour is drawn specks, not a grain filter, because the harvest names flour on the peel, not print, film, or a dusty paper surface.

## Visual system

Material: the page is proved dough skin, because the 41-year starter is stretched into the one loaf you are looking down on.

Light: the shop window at 6am, because that is when the door opens and the loaf is judged. The field is morning-bright dough. Night is a darker shade of the same skin, not a black room. The oven is hot off-screen. It does not glow on the page.

Palette, five hex values from that dough, in that morning light:

- `#EAD7B0` flour, because the peel's dust and the noon film are this pale.
- `#C9843F` crumb, because the opened score shows this tan interior.
- `#6B3A22` crust, because the 11pm bake browns the edge this far.
- `#2A160F` score, because the lame slit is this dark before it opens.

Type: display face Anton, file from `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Anton&family=Newsreader:opsz,wght@6..72,400;6..72,600&display=swap`, 104px. The swell stays in the dough, not in the letters. Newsreader belongs here because the village note is read in a text face, the way a feeding note is read, while the slash stays the display.

Picture rule: material swatch, accepted. The picture depicts a close crop of one boule: skin, flour specks, steam blisters, crumb at the cut, crust at the rim, one score, one ear. No people, no street, no icon set.

Motion: one family, Spring, `back.out(1.4)` inside the release beat only, duration 0.5s, which sits in the dealt 0.35 to 0.6s range. Scroll maps through that beat once. Repeated controls (the year switches) are 120ms and do not bounce, because a switch is not the poke.

Grade: morning window on one dough. Every shape uses only the five hex values above. `GRADE:` in the page names it. A missing photo of the village jar is a drawn stand-in marked placeholder, same five hex values. No second grade.

## Objects

The close crop means there is no row of cards. Each object differs.

- The night bake is the dough skin. Element `dough-skin`, `data-object="dough"`. It changes state: slack at 11pm, pressed, sprung with the ear up, then a flour ghost at noon. Size of the ear and the dimple change with that state.
- The lame cut is the score. Element `score-cut`, `data-object="score"`. It differs from a shut slit to an open ear. One loaf, one cut, not a pattern of slashes.
- The 41-year starter is the wet well. Element `starter-well`, `data-object="starter-well"`. Ring count differs: 1 on the village day, 20 on the long feed, 41 this morning. The center copy changes with the rings.
- The morning's loaves are bases on the rack. Element `noon-rack`, `data-object="rack"`. Three bases, sample bake, different widths (narrow, wide, medium), different blister counts, different browning. Taken loaves become flour outlines. Gaps between them are uneven.
- The control is the peel. Element `peel-bar`, `data-object="peel"`. A worn long board. The thumb sits at the hour the visitor chooses. It is the range, 44px tall.
- The village source is the jar stand-in. Element `village-jar`, `data-object="jar"`. One chip on the rim, placeholder label, words on the jar, not beside it.
- Budapest is the flour stamp. Element `pest-stamp`, `data-object="stamp"`. Pressed, rotated a few degrees, smaller than the score so it does not cover the cut.

## Genericness

Similar brief: a train-station croissant shop that also opens early and sells out of the morning's tray.

The part I would have written for them is a pale dough field plus an early clock. That part changes here. The field is one sourdough skin with a single lame score and an ear, not a stack of laminated layers. The clock is the 11pm oven, the 6am door, and a noon bare board, not a general "baked this morning" line. The 41-year well has no croissant version.

Escaping into cream paper, an italic serif, or a black page with one bright accent would still fail. This plan stays on the dealt world, The verb, and on dough colors because the hero is dough.

The dealt world stays. Motion and picture rule stay.
