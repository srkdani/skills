# Kovász sample

Deal accepted: world "Walk into the place", motion "Snap", picture "Screenprint".

Rejected worlds:
- Two states, one clip. A wipe would turn the night into a before and after widget. The visitor needs the door, the oven, and the rack as rooms they can stand in.
- Operable document. The thing in the hand is a loaf, not a log. A feeding chart would hide the oven wall and the bare wire.

## Harvest

1. The bake starts at 11pm, while Budapest is dark. Ritual and time.
2. The shop door opens at 6am. Time.
3. The starter is 41 years old. History.
4. The starter was carried from the baker's grandmother's village. Origin. The village is not named here.
5. The racks sell out by noon. Status.
6. The shop is in Budapest. Place.
7. Kovász is the Hungarian word for the leaven itself. Vocabulary.
8. The oven is hot at 11pm while the street outside is cold. Temperature. Physical.
9. At 6am the crust has cracked and the shop smells sour and baked. Smell and sound. Physical.
10. Flour dusts the bench during the night mix. Touch and sight. Physical.
11. The starter lives in a jar fed with flour and water. Material. Physical.
12. A lame scores the round before the oven, and the cut opens as oven spring. Tool and process. Physical.
13. By noon the racks are bare wire. Sight. Physical.
14. The open door at 6am lets in low street light against the oven's heat. Light. Physical.
15. The enemy is arriving after noon, when the smell is left and the wire is empty.
16. Someone at 6 meets full racks. Someone at 12 meets wire.

At least six are physical or sensory: 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14. None of these is a virtue.

## Truths

1. The bake starts at 11pm and the door opens at 6am. Rival: a daytime café bakery that mixes after dawn and sells through the afternoon. They do not run the oven as the night itself.
2. The starter is 41 years old and was carried from a grandmother's village. Rival: a bakery that uses commercial yeast, or a starter dried and bought last year. They do not have this jar.
3. The racks are empty by noon. Rival: a supermarket bakery that restocks until evening. They do not end the day on bare wire.

## Concepts

1. The site is three rooms of one night, where the 11pm oven, the 6am counter, and the noon wire are rooms you walk through and scrolling spends the bread. Rival: a supermarket bakery. Their aisle does not start as a night oven or end as bare wire at noon. Keep.
2. The site is the carried jar held up to a lamp, where each bubble is a year and tipping it plays the village trip. Rival: a fermentation lab. A jar held to a lamp still fits them. FAIL. Replaced: The site is one paper bag printed with the night, where shaking it empties the rack by noon. Rival: a grocery delivery service. A bag you shake still fits them. FAIL.
3. The site is the 6am doorway shown again at noon, where the same sill is full and then wire. Rival: a lunch counter that sells out. Two pictures of a doorway still fit them. FAIL.

Only concept 1 survives the swap. Concepts 2 and 3 were replaced and still fit a rival, so they stay failed.

## Pick

Concept 1, the three rooms of one night. It is the dealt world: you stand on a floor, face a wall, and use a tool. The visitor sentence comes true because the first scroll splits the round, the jar in the foreground can be tipped, and a later room lets them pull loaves off the wire.

Rejected: the jar as the whole site. A jar you hold is not a room you walk into, and merging it with the rooms would be two concepts. The jar stays the foreground crop of the night room.

Rejected: the double doorway. That is the clip world, already rejected. It would hide the bench and the lame.

## Visual system

- Material: screenprinted soot plaster, flour, and crust, because the night wall is smoked plaster, the bench is floured, and the dealt picture is flat ink. Crumb holes are drawn in the round. This is not a flat cream field.
- Light: the oven mouth is the lamp in the first room, because the bake starts at 11pm in a dark shop. The 6am room is brighter, because the door is open and street light comes in. The noon room keeps that daylight on bare wire. The oven ink is the only fire.
- Palette: `#2A211C` soot plaster, because the night wall behind the oven is smoked. `#E7D7B8` flour, because the bench and the morning wall are dusted flour, and this is the paper of the print. `#7A3B24` crust, because the second ink is the baked crust. `#E25C28` oven mouth, because the only lamp at 11pm is the open oven, and this color is only that fire.
- Type: display "Big Shoulders Display" 800, file `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Big+Shoulders+Display:wght@800&family=Outfit:wght@400;600;700&display=swap`, because the oven door is a tall narrow slab and the sign has to fit the stile in condensed painted letters. Body "Outfit" at 18px. Display `clamp(4rem, 12vw, 11rem)`, which is at least four times the body, because the door sign is read from the street. On a narrow door the floor is 4rem so the arch, the round, and the bag stay in the first view. Tracking `-0.03em`, because the condensed face can take it. One type idea: the door sign. No italic.
- Picture: screenprint kept. Two or three flat inks, large shapes, a second crust pass offset 2px. No photographic texture. The picture depicts the oven wall, the floured bench, the jar, the counter, and the wire rack. There is no photograph of the grandmother's village, so that state of the jar is marked placeholder.
- Motion: Snap kept. `power3.out`. Repeated taps under 150ms, because a lame flicks and a loaf leaves the rack in one short move. The signature may run longer, because it explains the night bake: scroll from 0 to 30 percent scrubs the round open. Menus and repeats are under 150ms. No bounce, and no `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`.
- Sound: none.
- Grade: one screenprint. Flour, crust, and oven ink, second crust pass 2px off, on every room. Named in the page comment.
