Questions

Short answers about this sample page.

What is Keel?

Keel is a sample product, not a real company. It is a sample invoicing tool for independent design studios. It turns finished jobs into invoices.

Are the prices real?

No. Prices and figures are sample data. The sample prices are Starter at $18 per month, Studio at $42 per month, and Practice at $79 per month.

How do the sample plans differ?

Starter covers an invoice from a finished job, a PDF, and paid or unpaid status. Studio adds client records, retainer invoices, and overdue status. Practice adds a shared workspace, invoice templates, and export of invoice records.

What is North Room?

North Room is the sample studio name used on this page. It is not a customer list, and it is not a quote.
